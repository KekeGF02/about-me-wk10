Introducing Me

A fun fact is that my full first name is Keiwana, but officially I've been going by Keke since 10th grade. Twenty-four years ago, I was born and have been raised in the Bronx. I pursued journalism because I want to understand the world around me through writing and speaking with different types of people, and I would like to stay local as a reporter.
